{%- comment -%} Preview-card image tags, shared by every layout. Open Graph plus Twitter Cards is the whole convention -- Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack, Discord, Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, Mastodon, Bluesky and Teams all read one or both. There is no third vocabulary worth carrying. This site has no jekyll-seo-tag, so nothing else emits og:image or twitter:card; unlike the Orange Line site's split, this include owns them outright. The layouts still write og:title, og:description, og:url and og:type themselves, since those are per-page. The picture is the masthead train on its rail -- the same header a visitor lands on -- built by assets/branding/sandbox/make_branding.py. Defaults live in _config.yml under `social:`.
