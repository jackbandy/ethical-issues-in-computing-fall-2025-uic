# The Eternal Recurrence

Would you live the same life you are living now, over and over, for eternity? This is a thought experiment posed by Friedrich Nietzsche, who specifies "there will be nothing new about it," and that "every pain and every pleasure, and every thought and sigh, and everything unspeakably small and great in your life must come back to you and all in the same series and sequence." This means experiencing the same joys and pleasures, and also the same failures and disappointments as your current life.

Would you live the same life you are living right now, on an infinite loop?

Source: [Kevin Aho, "Existentialism", The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/existentialism/#NietOver)
