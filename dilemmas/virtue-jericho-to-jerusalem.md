# Jericho to Jerusalem

You are walking across campus to get to a class in which you are scheduled to give a presentation. You are running slightly late and will need to hurry to arrive on time. As you hurry along, you pass a lecture center building and see a distressed person slumped against a wall, coughing and groaning.

_What do you do?_
