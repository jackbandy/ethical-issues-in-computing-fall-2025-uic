# Plug In

Suppose a company called PearLink offers a shiny new system that includes a headset, a sensory deprivation tank, and a handful of (painless) biochemical links around your body.

The system gives you any experience you want.

When connected, you can have the experience of "writing a great poem or bringing about world peace or loving someone and being loved in return. You can experience the felt pleasures of these things, how they feel 'from the inside.' You can program your experiences for tomorrow, or this week, or this year, or even for the rest of your life. If your imagination is impoverished, you can use the library of suggestions extracted from biographies and enhanced by novelists and psychologists" and polished by LLMs to align with your personal preferences and history.

_How, if at all, would you interact with this machine?_
