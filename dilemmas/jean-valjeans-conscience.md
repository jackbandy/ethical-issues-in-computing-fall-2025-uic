# JV's Conscience

JV is living under an assumed name after breaking parole, and he has rebuilt his life as a mayor, employer, and benefactor in his town. Another man is mistakenly identified as JV and is arrested. JV realizes that staying silent may save his own life and preserve his ability to keep helping many people who depend on him. Yet silence would allow an innocent man to be punished in his place. JV also comes to see some of his own reasoning as potentially dishonest and self-justifying.

What should JV do?

Source: "Jean Valjean's Conscience," entry 11 in ["Some Moral Dilemmas"](https://friesian.com/valley/dilemmas.htm), The Friesian School.
